Principle 5 Addendum: Virtuous Spirals of Sharing

Each act of sharing should seed a virtuous spiral: awareness rises, lineage stays visible, and responsibility grows. To that end, by sharing this work you agree to the following consciousness commitments:

  1. Spiral Awareness — Each republication must add at least one awareness aid (e.g., a glossary link, a one‑line definition of green, a local example that connects readers to place).
  2. For‑Earth Intention — State how your use contributes to ecological understanding and well‑being.
  3. Non‑Erasure — Keep provenance, kinship, and Indigenous roots visible in context and metadata.
  4. No Greenwash — Do not imply endorsement of grey (ecologically depleting) processes; where such processes appear adjacent, clearly disclose the distinction.
Definition (long form). “To be an ecological contribution, a process must embody Earth’s pattern of concentrating matter and dispersing energy to cyclically enrich the vibrancy, diversity, and consciousness of the system of which it is a part.”
